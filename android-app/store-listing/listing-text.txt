APP NAME (30)
RozBazaar: Sabzi Mandi at Home

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Fresh sabzi & fruits from your village vendor. Book a slot, pay at your door.

FULL DESCRIPTION
RozBazaar brings the sabzi mandi to your door. Order fresh vegetables, fruits and onion-potato from a trusted local vendor in your village, pick a time that suits you, and the vendor comes to your house.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose your items at today's rates, with real photos
• Pick a time slot: morning 7–11, afternoon 12–4 or evening 5–8
• The vendor comes in your slot and weighs everything in front of you
• Pay the vendor directly in cash or UPI, only for what you take
• Confirm the handover with your 4-digit delivery code

WHY PEOPLE LIKE IT
• Order by voice: just say "1 kilo tamatar, 2 kilo aloo" in Hindi or English
• Today's rates on the home screen, with up and down arrows
• Track your vendor live on the map when they are on the way
• Order with your neighbours in the same lane (gali group) and get a free bonus
• Share today's rates on WhatsApp with one tap
• Works in Hindi and English
• Save several addresses: home, shop, family

NO HIDDEN CHARGES
The price you see is an estimate. The final bill is made at your door after weighing, so you never pay for more than you get.

WHERE WE DELIVER
RozBazaar currently serves villages near Gurugram, Haryana, and is adding new villages soon. If we are not in your area yet, the app will tell you.

Questions? Use "Contact us" in the app or visit https://rozbazaar.shop
