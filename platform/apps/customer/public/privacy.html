RozBazaar (rozbazaar.shop and the RozBazaar Android app) helps people in the villages of the Pataudi and Haileymandi area book fresh vegetables, fruits and onion-potato from a local vendor, who comes to their door in the chosen time slot. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and your choices. RozBazaar is run by Vedanshu Rathi, Haryana, India.

In short: we collect only what is needed to deliver your order. We never sell your data, we show no ads, and we never take card or bank details — you pay the vendor directly in cash or UPI.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your name and mobile number. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Delivery address: the addresses you save (village, house number, street, landmark, a name like "Home" or "Shop") and, if you allow it, the exact map location (latitude/longitude) of your door.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my location" or "Allow location", your phone's GPS position is used to find your village and to place the pin on the map. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Orders: the items, quantities, prices, time slot, notes for the vendor, the final bill, how you paid (cash or UPI — recorded by the vendor, not the payment itself), ratings and complaints.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a notification token for your device, and whether a notification reached your phone and whether you opened it.
  • Messages: anything you send us through "Contact us".
  • Settings: your language (English or Hindi) and favourite items.
  • Usage: an anonymous count of which page was opened (no name, phone or location attached).

We do not collect card numbers, bank or UPI details, contacts, photos, SMS or call logs.

2. How we use it

  • To take your booking and pass it to the vendor who serves your village in that slot.
  • So the vendor can find your door, call you if needed, make your bill and confirm delivery with your 4-digit delivery code.
  • To send you order updates (confirmed, vendor on the way, at your door, bill ready, completed), slot reminders and, occasionally, RozBazaar announcements.
  • To help with complaints, disputes and customer support.
  • To improve the service (for example, which villages need more vendors).
  • To prevent misuse of the service and to follow the law.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your vendor: for your order only — your name, phone number, delivery address, map location, items and notes.
  • The RozBazaar team: our admin and staff, to run and support the service.
  • Service providers that run the app for us:
    • Supabase (database and login; servers in Mumbai, India)
    • Hostinger (website hosting)
    • Google (Google sign-in and delivery of notifications)
    • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim and Esri (map tiles and turning your map pin into a street name — they receive only the map coordinates, not your name or phone)
  • Authorities: only if the law requires it.

We never sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and addresses while your account is active. Order records are kept for accounting and dispute purposes for up to 3 years. When you delete an address that was used in a past order, it is hidden from you but kept with that order. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can say no — just type your address instead. You can change this in your phone's settings any time.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in your phone's settings any time.
  • Addresses and profile: you can add, edit and delete them in the app under "My account".
  • Access, correction, deletion: you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data. This is your right under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

6. How to delete your account

Email vedanshurathi@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my RozBazaar account", or use "Contact us" in the app, and include the mobile number of your account. We will confirm and delete your account and personal data within 30 days.

7. Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted so that each person can see only their own information, and vendors only the orders they deliver. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and will inform you if a breach affects you.

8. Children

RozBazaar is meant for adults placing household orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
