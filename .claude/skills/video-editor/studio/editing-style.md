## Rejected — never repeat
- 2026-10 · Constant zoom in/out, beat-pulse zooms, shakes: "so much zoom in zoom out… a person can not focus".
- 2026-10 · Messy real background behind the phone screen: show only the clean screen, animated.
- 2026-10 · Busy covers with many competing elements; over-processed "plastic" face (first neon "15" cover: "so bad").
- 2026-10 · Thumbnail text over the face; text at the top when he asked for it at the bottom ("phone upside is good but put text downside").
- 2026-11 · Fabricated or unclear claims — keep to what he said or what sources confirm.

## Defaults that follow from the above
- Camera moves: slow, eased, purposeful; at most one gentle push per shot.
- Every 2–4 s something changes (cut, graphic, card), not the zoom level.
- Voiceover: his own voice when available; otherwise Kokoro `hm_psi` (offline), speed ~1.0–1.08.
- Always deliver: MP4 + cover + ready caption (+ sources for news).
