# Character bible: Avir (Unfiltered Avir)

**Status:** name and brand locked (2026-11-07).
- Visual identity: `reference/avir_reference_approved.webp` (front, side, back, close-up). This image is the
  source of truth for his face, hair and beard.
- 3D model: `../host3d/dist/host.glb` (v1 stand-in: rig, face controls and outfits work; the likeness is still
  below the reference, see host3d/README.md).

## Who he is
A virtual creator, a young Indian man in his early twenties. Curious, relatable and honest. He tries new things,
takes on challenges, tests AI tools, builds projects and shares what he finds like an independent YouTuber,
including the failures. His channel can grow beyond AI into technology, experiments and real-world experiences.

## Fixed identity (never changes)
- Realistic young Indian man, warm medium-brown skin, defined but natural jawline.
- Short, well-groomed black beard and moustache.
- Natural short black hair with soft volume on top, swept up and back with a light, natural fringe.
- Dark brown expressive eyes, thick natural brows, friendly and confident expression.
- Face, hair and beard stay identical in every video.

## Changes naturally
- **Outfits:** polished casual: plain tee with an overshirt (signature: charcoal overshirt + cream tee + dark trousers
  + white sneakers), casual shirts, tasteful hoodies, light jackets. Festive wear for festival episodes.
- **Also changes:** expressions, poses, locations (his room/studio as the recurring set, streets, cafés, metro, rooftops).

## Voice
Kokoro `hm_omega` (Hindi male), speed 1.0 → tempo 1.05. Scripts for the voice are written in Devanagari, and every
line is checked with Whisper. Captions mix Devanagari with everyday English words in Latin script.

## Trust rules
1. Avir is a **virtual character**. Never say or imply he is a real human. Use YouTube's altered/synthetic-content
   setting and Instagram's AI label where they apply.
2. Tool demos and experiments use **genuine screen recordings and real results**.
3. Animated or simulated scenes are clearly separated from real actions and outcomes (on-screen label such as
   "animated scene" / "real result" when it could be confused).
4. No invented facts, results, numbers or testimonials.

## Continuity rules
- Every render uses the same model file (`host.glb`) or, for 2D art, the approved reference.
- Text never covers his face. Captions sit in the lower third.
- Outfits change between episodes or story scenes, not mid-sentence.
