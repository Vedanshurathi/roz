# Haryana Government Schemes Relevant to RozBazaar

Researched 2026-09-13. Primary source: startupharyana.gov.in, haryanait.gov.in, startupindia.gov.in, msme.haryana.gov.in.

## 1. Startup Haryana (State Startup Policy 2022) — the main one

**Eligibility to register:**
- Must first be DPIIT-recognized as a "Startup" (via startupindia.gov.in — free, ~10 days). RozBazaar likely qualifies since it's an app/tech-platform-based model, not pure resale — but pure trading businesses can get rejected by DPIIT, so frame the application around the tech/logistics platform, not "we sell groceries."
- Registered office in Haryana + Haryana Udyam Memorandum (HUM).
- Business must not fall in Haryana's "restrictive list" (HEEP 2020).
- Can't be double-dipping incentives already claimed elsewhere.

**Benefits once registered (all via investharyana.in portal):**
- Seed funding: up to ₹10 lakh (idea validation, prototype, PoC, market research, travel, training) — evaluated quarterly.
- Lease rental subsidy: 30% reimbursement (45% if woman-founded), capped ₹5 lakh/year — for space in incubators/IT parks/industrial clusters.
- Net SGST reimbursement: 50% for 7 years once you're doing commercial (billed) operations, capped at 100% of fixed capital investment.
- Patent cost reimbursement: 100% up to ₹25 lakh.
- Acceleration program fees: up to ₹2.5 lakh/year.
- Cloud storage reimbursement: 75% up to ₹2.5 lakh/year for 5 years, IF hosted on a Haryana-based data center.
- Non-fiscal: mentorship, incubator access, fair/exhibition participation.

## 2. Udyam (MSME) Registration — free, do this regardless

Free registration at udyamregistration.gov.in. Unlocks:
- Collateral-free loans via CGTMSE credit guarantee (up to ₹10 Cr as of current scheme).
- Priority-sector lending / lower interest rates from banks.
- Protection against delayed payments from buyers.
- Eligibility feeder for various Haryana MSME subsidy schemes (interest subvention, stamp duty exemption, power tariff subsidy) under Haryana Enterprises & Employment Policy 2020 — mainly aimed at manufacturing/fixed-capital-investment businesses, so less directly useful for RozBazaar's software/logistics model, but worth having Udyam status either way.

## 3. Har Hith Store Yojana — adjacent, not a direct fit

Run by Haryana Agro Industries Corporation (HAICL): sets up individual franchise grocery stores in villages/towns, guarantees ₹15,000/month income to the franchisee for first 6 months, gives collateral-free Mudra loans. This is aimed at individuals running a single physical shop, not a startup/platform — doesn't fit RozBazaar's model directly, but worth knowing it exists (potential local competitor/partner network, or a franchisee could double as a fulfillment node).

## Suggested sequence
1. DPIIT Startup recognition (free, startupindia.gov.in).
2. Udyam/MSME registration (free).
3. Register on Startup Haryana portal + get HUM.
4. Apply for Seed Funding (₹10L) as the first real cash — this is the one most worth chasing early.
5. Once revenue/GST billing starts, track SGST reimbursement.
